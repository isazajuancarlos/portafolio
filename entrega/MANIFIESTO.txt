HOW TO VERIFY WHAT YOU RECEIVE

  sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
  chmod +x verificar-firma
  ./verificar-firma verificar "$(cat CLAVE-PUBLICA.txt)" \
      quipu-0.11.1-614f46c.tar.gz quipu-0.11.1-614f46c.tar.gz.firma

  A good delivery prints "FIRMA VALIDA" and exits 0. A tampered one prints
  "FIRMA INVALIDA - NO INSTALES ESTE ARCHIVO" and exits 1. The signature is
  hybrid and validates only if both Ed25519 and ML-DSA-87 validate.
